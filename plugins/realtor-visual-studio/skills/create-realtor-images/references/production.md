# Visual production
## Prompt recipe
Create one [ratio] image in [style]. Show [subject/action] in [setting, finishes, season, location cues]. Use [camera, framing, lighting, tone]. Leave [caption area] and keep essential subjects centrally crop-safe. Preserve [reference geometry and identity]. Include [required elements], exclude [text/logos/artifacts/unwanted elements]. Respect [truthfulness constraints].
Put exclusions in main prompt if no negative-prompt parameter exists. Do not invent tool parameters. Store the actual submitted prompt.

## Diversity
Prioritize script relevance. Mix wide establishing, medium and detail shots; interiors/exteriors; process/planning scenes; optional people and quiet CTA background. Aim at four shot types for 10 and six for 20. Avoid more than three near-identical shots per 10 or five per 20 unless continuity calls for it. Do not force people into architecture-only content. Keep cohesive photographic style while changing useful subjects. Depict plausible local homes instead of generic mansions unless requested. Represent households naturally without stereotypes.

## Reference continuity
Use one master image per same-home group and edit with actual reference. Preserve camera, floor plan, island position, doors/windows, ceiling height and structure. For 1980s interior: oak cabinetry, laminate counters, linoleum flooring, brass fixtures, white appliances, mild dated wear when requested. For modernization change only requested finishes. For slightly neglected exterior: mildly overgrown lawn/shrubs and dated weathered finishes, unchanged roof/windows/lot/driveway. No severe damage or structural defects unless requested. A fresh unrelated generation is not the same home.
Four alternatives means four individual files. Make a quartered comparison only when explicitly requested as an additional asset.

## Accuracy
Fictional educational homes may illustrate possibilities; store AI-generated illustration disclosure. For real listings use actual sources and confirmed facts. Never invent renovations, amenities, view, floor plan, history or condition. Identify virtual staging and conceptual renovations, carrying disclosure into suggested post copy. Avoid fake identifiable local landmarks, prices, statistics, returns and guarantees. Flag unverified factual claims; use a verified chart/design workflow for exact data and typography instead of image-generated numbers.

## QC and revisions
Inspect architecture, stairs, cabinet alignment, reflections, appliance proportions, skin/hands, perspective and lighting. Score relevance, realism and composition 1–5 in QA notes; aim at least 4, explain subjective tradeoffs. Verify continuity against reference. Reject severe defects.
Retry defective scenes at most twice without new input, then report limitation and keep usable files.
Translate feedback explicitly:
- Too luxurious: attainable homes, practical furnishings, ordinary lot.
- Looks fake: natural light, restrained contrast, believable textures.
- Too repetitive: vary action, shot and script-linked subject.
- Same room but older: reference edit, retain layout, change specified finishes.
- Warmer modern: soft palette, updated materials, less sterile light.
- Caption space: recompose around uncluttered area.
Preserve original intent unless creator requests a new concept.
