# Production output and style

## Professional style
Design for 1080×1920 export when supported. Prioritize clarity, credible imagery and clean composition. Use no face-to-camera narration, lip-sync avatar or distracting gimmicks. Generic people in B-roll only when creator permits; faceless may mean no identifiable faces, so ask if ambiguous.
Default caption styling: legible sans-serif, high contrast, consistent lower-middle placement, maximum two lines and roughly 3–7 words per phrase. Use one restrained highlight colour for active word; avoid bouncing typography. Maintain readability, avoid important visual subjects. Use conservative central safe-area guidance until current target platform UI margins are verified; do not describe fixed percentages as universal rules.
Subtle pan/zoom may add movement to stills; avoid excessive zoom, rapid flicker or meaningless transitions. Voice remains clear and primary. Optional music only with creator direction and appropriate rights; use app mixing controls without claiming universal loudness settings.
Use actual approved brand palette/logo when supplied. No watermark or counterfeit logos, misleading renovated listings, invented charts or data.

## Platform adapter output
Give this compact matrix:
Capability | Verified available / unavailable / unknown | Production action
Rows: voice choice/clone, speech export, word timing, highlighted captions, image/B-roll import, shot duration controls, 9:16 export, accessibility caption track.
Use official documentation for actual menus/plan restrictions; cite sources when researched. Never invent provider voice IDs, tools or UI buttons. For an unavailable operation, offer only one minimal verified workaround plus creator choice.
If generator offers only 6-second clips, generate then trim usable visual to 3–5 seconds in editor; do not claim generator directly obeyed shot duration. No fabricated automatic integration.

## Narration instructions
Voice mode: own clone / preset / original designed voice.
Record service/profile if accessible, language/accent, pronunciation, calm/warm/confident tone, natural pace, pauses. Keep real identity out of prompt unless creator supplies/authorizes it.
Provide exact script separately from stage directions; spoken narration must not read production notes.
Render narration first, export audio, retrieve duration/timestamps, then finalize visuals.
Do not introduce a visible avatar; no video likeness creation needed.

## Paste-ready master prompt template
"You are my faceless reel production assistant. I use [verified app/version]. Use the attached final script [version] and narration [audio/version, or pending]. Produce a professional clean 9:16 reel/story for [platform/audience/goal]. Voice: [authorized own clone/preset/original voice specification]. Use images/B-roll only, no talking avatar. Every visual shot must last 3–5 seconds, including opening/CTA. Calculate count from actual duration; until audio is supplied mark all times estimated. Maintain complete uncut narration, verbatim captions and word-aligned active highlighting. Caption highlight needs supported overlay/editor features; provide a separate accessibility subtitle track where supported. Follow attached storyboard/prompts/brand constraints. Report unavailable features and propose a supported minimal workaround. Deliver a scene table, individual image/video prompts, narration instructions, caption plan, verified assembly steps and QA. Do not claim video rendered or words aligned without evidence. Ask only missing production-critical questions and request improvements after review."

## Completion checklist
All narration mapped; no visual unsupported claims.
9:16 framing and platform-safe caption placement.
Every shot 3–5 seconds; gaps/overlaps explicitly accounted for.
Voice correctly chosen and authorized; speech intelligible and natural.
Captions verbatim; active-word highlight matches actual speech.
Final timeline equals intended duration and all narration included.
No on-camera avatar; images/video clips consistent with brief.
Instructions distinguish estimated timing from verified alignment.
Creator can identify next app action and outstanding inputs.
